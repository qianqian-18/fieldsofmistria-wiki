Fields of Mistria characters are villagers with schedules, preferences, conversations and requests. Players can locate residents on the map, speak with them and improve relationships through gifts. Some characters are romance candidates, while many others support the wider story of rebuilding Mistria.

## How villager schedules work

Residents move through the town according to their own routines. The map displays real-time positions, reducing the need to search every location manually. Birthdays and special events are shown at the start of the day.

The approved research does not provide a complete schedule for every season, weather condition or story stage. Exact timetable pages therefore remain 待确认. A future schedule guide should state the game version because updates can change routines.

## Conversation and community

Players can listen to villagers interact with one another as well as speak to them directly. Reviewers praised the writing and the combination of sincerity and humor. A Friday inn gathering was also highlighted for making the town feel like a connected community.

These observations describe the reception found in the supplied research. They do not provide a line-by-line dialogue archive. Dialogue choices, event prerequisites and every conversation variation need separate verification.

## Friendship and gifts

Relationships improve through conversation and gift-giving. Villagers have preferences, and quests may also begin after interacting with them. The supplied material confirms the system but does not include a reliable loved-gift table for each character.

Searches for March, Juniper, Hayden, Caldarus, Eiland, Celine, Valen, Balor, Olric and other residents appear in the keyword file. A keyword alone is not proof of a character's schedule or preferred item. Detailed character pages will remain unpublished until their facts are supported.

## Confirmed names in the supplied material

Caldarus is identified as the dragon statue connected to Mistria's magic. The community wiki navigation excerpt also names several residents and locations, including March at the Blacksmith's Shop, Valen at the Clinic, Juniper at the Bathhouse and Hayden at Sweetwater Farm. This confirms those navigation associations only.

The excerpt also lists Celine, Balor, Ryis, Terithia, Errol, Josephine, Nora and Elsie. It does not provide complete biographies, ages, gift lists or heart-event requirements. Those attributes remain 待确认.

## Character customization

The player character can be customized from the beginning, including name, birthday and pronouns. Romance candidates are not locked by player gender. Farm and house customization sit alongside character personalization.

Version 1.0 is announced to add more customization and to extend romance progression. Exact cosmetic inventories and unlock requirements are not provided in the approved sources. This wiki will separate confirmed update features from later item-level guides.

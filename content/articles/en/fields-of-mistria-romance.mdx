Fields of Mistria romance lets players build relationships through conversation and gifts without locking candidates by the player character's gender. The supplied research confirms heart progression, dating and announced marriage features. It does not contain a complete candidate-by-candidate romance walkthrough.

## Building relationships

Villagers have schedules and preferences, and their current positions can appear on the map. Talking and giving gifts can improve relationship status. Requests and festivals create additional reasons to interact with the community.

The source material does not include verified loved gifts for each candidate. Searches such as Juniper loved gifts or Caldarus gifts appear in the keyword list, but search demand is not evidence. Item-specific gift claims remain To be confirmed.

## Hearts and dates

A supplied video transcript says the early-access cap is eight hearts and version 1.0 raises romanceable characters to ten hearts. The transcript also says dates will award more heart points in 1.0. These details are tied to the announced update context.

Exact point values, cooldowns and every event trigger are not included in the approved research. The wiki will not invent heart thresholds beyond the explicitly supplied information. Players should verify changing mechanics in current official notes.

## Marriage and children

The version 1.0 information in the supplied research lists proposal and wedding events, marriage and children. One transcript says players may decline the opportunity to have a baby after marriage. This specific behavior comes from the transcript excerpt and should be checked against the final release notes.

## Choosing a romance

The supplied review research praises character writing, but “best romance” is a matter of player preference. No source establishes one candidate as objectively best. Players can choose based on dialogue, personality and events rather than a fabricated ranking.

For the same reason, this wiki will not label a gift route as fastest without verified point data. The reliable starting advice is to speak with residents, learn their schedules and use confirmed preferences when those sources become available.

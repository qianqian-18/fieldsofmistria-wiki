This Fields of Mistria wiki is an independent fan-made guide that organizes verified information about the game. It covers the farm, town, villagers, quests, skills, magic, mining, crafting, relationships and release information. Unsupported details are marked To be confirmed instead of being presented as fact.

## What you can find here

The home page provides a starting point for beginners, a verified game overview and quick access to published guides. Special Pages lists every article that currently has enough source support to publish. Search variants and spelling mistakes point toward canonical topics rather than creating repeated thin pages.

The first article set focuses on the game overview, release date, gameplay, characters, romance, the Stardew Valley comparison and Steam availability. A beginner guide explains the confirmed systems without inventing an optimal route. More specific character, gift, item and quest pages remain To be confirmed when the supplied research does not contain enough detail.

## How facts are selected

The approved research includes a Wikipedia-derived overview, an excerpt from the community wiki and two YouTube transcript excerpts. Information is filtered to remove advertisements, transcript filler, duplicate statements and unsupported assumptions. Creator opinions are described as opinions rather than converted into objective facts.

Time-sensitive facts need a date or version context. For example, early-access systems may differ from the announced 1.0 version. When sources conflict, the more authoritative supplied source is preferred; unresolved conflicts remain To be confirmed.

## Main guide categories

Gameplay coverage includes farming, ranching, fishing, mining, crafting, combat and magic. Community coverage includes villagers, friendship, romance, quests, festivals, town rank and museum donations. Platform coverage currently focuses on Windows PC, Steam and Steam Deck compatibility.

The community wiki excerpt lists many more categories, including tools, equipment, cosmetics, crops, cooking, forageables, insects, furniture and locations. Their existence is verified as navigation information, but detailed values and item-by-item instructions are not automatically copied into this site. Each detailed guide needs its own supporting research.

## Languages

The interface supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, French, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Spanish. The language set follows the official first-wave localization announcement for version 1.0. When a verified translation is not yet available, the English article appears with a visible fallback notice.

Translation must preserve the meaning of approved facts. It must not add character preferences, item prices, unlock requirements or redemption codes that are absent from the source material. This rule applies equally to every language.

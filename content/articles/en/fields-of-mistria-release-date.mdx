The verified Fields of Mistria release date for version 1.0 is August 5, 2026. The game first entered Steam early access for Windows on August 5, 2024, exactly two years earlier. A playable demo was available from June 5 to June 17, 2024.

## Early access launch

Fields of Mistria launched in early access on Steam on August 5, 2024. The early version already included the farm, town, villagers and the main mixture of farming, exploration, relationships and magic. NPC Studio continued updating the game during development.

The first major update arrived on November 18, 2024 according to the supplied research. Later patch and roadmap details changed the available systems over time. Any guide that discusses a specific mechanic should therefore state the relevant version when that information is known.

## Full 1.0 release

NPC Studio announced August 5, 2026 as the full 1.0 date. The supplied official information says the town restoration storyline becomes completable, romance progression reaches ten hearts and marriage and children are added. Additional quests, rewards, skill perks, customization items and balancing are also listed for 1.0.

One supplied transcript says existing saves do not need to be restarted for 1.0 and that previously earned achievements should appear automatically. This claim comes from a video transcript rather than the pasted official FAQ, so players should verify it against current official patch notes before relying on it. The restart requirement is therefore presented with source context rather than as an unconditional guarantee.

## Language support at launch

The first wave of beta localizations announced for 1.0 includes Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, French, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Spanish. English is the language listed on the current Steam store material in the supplied research. German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese are not part of the confirmed first-wave list provided here.

Language availability can change after release. This wiki supports the announced language set for navigation, but a wiki translation is separate from the game's official localization. Missing translated articles fall back to verified English content.

## Platform timing

The game is currently verified in the supplied material for Windows PC through Steam and for Steam Deck compatibility. NPC Studio has said potential ports will be evaluated in the future. A confirmed Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PlayStation 5 or other console release date is not included in the approved sources and remains To be confirmed.

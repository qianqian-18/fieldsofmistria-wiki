Fields of Mistria begins with a damaged farm and a town recovering from an earthquake. Your immediate goal is to build a workable daily routine while helping Mistria recover. The supplied research confirms that farming, gathering, fishing, mining, crafting, quests, relationships and town rank all contribute to progress.

## Begin with the daily information

The game presents birthdays and special events at the start of each day. Use that information to decide whether the day should focus on farm work, a town event or meeting villagers. Exact first-day optimization values were not included in the supplied research, so any fixed schedule remains 待确认.

## Build a balanced routine

Traditional farm-life activities include planting, animal care, fishing, mining and crafting. The game also contains combat and magic, so progress is not limited to crop income. A balanced routine lets you improve the farm while continuing quests and town restoration.

## Use requests and quests

Mistria has a request board with quests that can be completed for rewards. Villager interactions and festivals can also provide quests. The supplied material does not verify a universal best order for every request, so prioritize tasks that match the items and time currently available.

## Learn skills and magic

Farming, fishing and gathering can provide essence that connects to a skill tree and useful abilities. Mana becomes available later and supports spells such as healing, restoring energy, growing crops or summoning rain. Exact unlock requirements for each spell are 待确认 in the provided research.

## Meet the villagers

Villagers have their own schedules and preferences, and the map can show their current positions. Relationships improve through conversation and gifts, while romance is available regardless of the player character's gender. Gift-by-gift preferences are not included in the approved source material and remain 待确认.

## Keep contributing to the town

The player can raise the town's rank and receive benefits while supporting the rebuilding effort. Museum donations can include flora, insects, artifacts and fish. Treat these systems as long-term progress rather than trying to finish every collection immediately.
